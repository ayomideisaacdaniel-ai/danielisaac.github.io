About

Founder with a builder's mindset.

I am an entrepreneur developing AfriPay Africa, a pan-African commerce technology company designed to connect consumers and businesses with verified sellers and suppliers.

My interests include entrepreneurship, digital commerce, business development, market research, copywriting and online business operations. I am especially interested in building practical, scalable solutions for African markets.

CURRENTLY BUILDING

AfriPay Africa

AfriPay is being designed as a connected commerce platform where consumers and businesses can discover products, communicate with sellers, make single or bulk purchases, request quotations, pay and coordinate delivery.

The long-term vision is to expand from Nigeria across Africa and eventually connect African buyers with global suppliers while helping African businesses reach international markets.
